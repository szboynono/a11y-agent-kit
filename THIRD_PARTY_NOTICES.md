## Dependency metadata

The [lockfile](package-lock.json) records dependency versions, integrity values and, where supplied, license identifiers.

- `axe-core` and `@axe-core/playwright` 4.14.0 are recorded as MPL-2.0.
- `playwright` and `playwright-core` 1.64.1 are recorded as Apache-2.0.
- `@modelcontextprotocol/server`, `@modelcontextprotocol/client` and `@modelcontextprotocol/core` 2.4.0 are recorded as Apache-2.0. Consult each package's LICENSE for its terms, including any terms applicable to legacy MIT contributions.
- `zod` 4.6.7 is recorded as MIT. The other locked library entries declare MIT or ISC.
